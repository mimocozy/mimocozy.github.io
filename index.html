About

Welcome to Mimocozy. A little corner where creativity, stories and cozy moments come to life.

Pages with a story

We make coloring books, illustrated stories and original collections, so each page feels like a small experience. Cozy scenes, adventures, romance, charming characters and magical worlds, made to keep you turning the page.

More than books

Mimocozy is a universe of characters, series and creative experiences. Each collection has its own personality, and all of them belong to the same world.

Your way

Take your favorite markers, pencils or pens, choose a page and make it completely yours. There is no wrong way to create. There is only yours.

A moment of your own

Coloring and slipping into a good story is more than passing the time. It is a way to slow down, create, relax and spend a few minutes in a world of your own.
