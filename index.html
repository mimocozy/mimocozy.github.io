About

Welcome to the world of Mimocozy. Cozy coloring books and little moments of joy.

A quieter kind of color

Mimocozy makes pages for slow evenings: original scenes, calm stories and room to breathe. Creativity, comfort and coloring, one small moment at a time.

The cat in the books

A sleepy cat keeps watch over the shelves. He would rather nap than rush, and every book begins with that same feeling: stay a little longer.

Different worlds

Seoul between scenes, a spooky-cute Halloween, the tiny world of Brambleboo, and Slow Life days by the sea, the fire or the garden. Different places, the same cozy feeling.

Made to color slowly

Each book is a short story you finish with pencils. Some are already on Amazon. Others are almost ready, waiting in Coming soon until the pages are published.
